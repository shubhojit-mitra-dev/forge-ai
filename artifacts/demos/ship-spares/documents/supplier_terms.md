# Marine Spares Supplier Terms & Pricing (Doc PROC-201)

## Section 1.1 Minimum Order & Bulk Discounts
- Orders of **10 to 24 units** of a single part number qualify for a **5% bulk discount** on unit price.
- Orders of **25 or more units** qualify for a **10% bulk discount** on unit price.

## Section 1.2 Expedited Lead Time
Standard supplier lead times are listed per part in `spares_catalogue.csv`. Emergency expedited manufacturing reduces lead time by **50%** (rounded up to whole days) and incurs an **18% expedite surcharge** on the subtotal.

## Section 1.3 Payment & Warranty
Standard payment terms are **Net 45 days** from bill of lading. OEM parts carry a **12-month** operational warranty.

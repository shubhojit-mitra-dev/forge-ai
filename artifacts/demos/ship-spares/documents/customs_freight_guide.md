# Port Customs & Freight Guide for Vessel Spares (Doc PROC-302)

## Section 2.1 Customs Duty & Clearance
- Imported marine spare parts incur a **7.5% customs duty** applied to the order subtotal (after any bulk discount).
- Standard customs clearance adds **3 business days** at Singapore (SGSIN) and **5 business days** at Rotterdam (NLRTM).

## Section 2.2 Freight Charges
- Standard sea/consolidated freight is **$150 USD** per shipment.
- Emergency air-freight for vessel-immobilized (critical) spares is a flat **$420 USD** per shipment.

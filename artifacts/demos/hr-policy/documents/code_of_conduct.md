# OceanLine Maritime — Shore-Staff Code of Conduct & Hybrid Work (Policy HR-305)

## Section 3.1 Remote & Hybrid Work
Shore staff who have completed their **3-month probation** may work remotely up to **2 days per week** (Tuesday/Thursday or manager-approved schedule). Core collaboration hours are **10:00–16:00 local time**.

## Section 3.2 Grievance Procedure
1. **Step 1 (Informal):** Discuss the concern directly with your immediate supervisor within 10 working days.
2. **Step 2 (Formal HR):** If unresolved, submit a written grievance to `hr-relations@oceanline.example` within 15 working days; HR convenes a panel within 7 working days.
3. **Step 3 (Appeal):** Final written appeal to the Chief People Officer within 10 working days of the panel decision.

## Section 3.3 Out-of-Scope Advice
HR policy assistants cannot provide personal legal advice, tax advice, or individual salary negotiation guidance.

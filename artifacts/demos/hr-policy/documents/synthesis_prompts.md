# Document Synthesis Prompts — Demo 1 (Shore-Staff HR Policy)

The three policy documents in `documents/` were synthesized to represent realistic maritime shore-staff HR policies with explicit section numbers, numeric entitlements, and clear out-of-scope boundaries:
1. `leave_policy.md`: Prompted for annual leave (22 days), sick leave (12 days with medical certificate after 3 days), parental leave, and carryover caps (5 days).
2. `travel_expense_policy.md`: Prompted for domestic/international per-diem rates, economy airfare rules, hotel caps ($180/night), and 14-day receipt submission deadlines.
3. `code_of_conduct.md`: Prompted for hybrid/remote work eligibility (2 days/week after probation), confidentiality, conflict of interest, and 3-step grievance escalation.

# OceanLine Maritime — Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy (Policy HR-204)

## Section 2.1 Airfare & Rail
All domestic and international flights under 6 hours must be booked in **Economy Class**. Flights exceeding 6 hours may be booked in Premium Economy only with VP approval.

## Section 2.2 Accommodation & Per-Diem
Standard hotel reimbursement is capped at **$180 USD per night** (excluding taxes). Meals and incidentals are reimbursed up to a daily per-diem of **$65 USD** domestic and **$95 USD** international. Alcohol and personal entertainment are non-reimbursable.

## Section 2.3 Claim Submission Deadline
All expense claims with itemized receipts must be submitted within **14 calendar days** of trip completion. Claims submitted after 30 days are automatically rejected.

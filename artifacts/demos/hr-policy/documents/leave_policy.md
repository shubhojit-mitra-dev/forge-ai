# OceanLine Maritime — Shore-Staff Leave Policy (Policy HR-101)

## Section 1.1 Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time shore staff receive **22 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.83 days per month. Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into Q1 of the following year; any balance above 5 days expires on March 31.

## Section 1.2 Leave Application & Notice
Requests for 1–3 consecutive days require **5 working days** advance notice via the HR portal. Requests for 4 or more consecutive days require **15 working days** advance notice and line-manager approval.

## Section 2.1 Sick & Medical Leave
Shore staff are entitled to **12 days** of paid sick leave per year. Absences exceeding **3 consecutive working days** require a licensed physician's medical certificate submitted within 48 hours of return.

## Section 3.1 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive **16 weeks** of paid parental leave; secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks** of paid leave within 6 months of birth or adoption.

## Section 4.1 Exclusions
This policy does not cover seafarer/vessel crew rotations (governed by MLC-2006) or compensation/salary reviews.
